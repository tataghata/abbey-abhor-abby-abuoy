# 020 · antistrophe

An answering turn, or an ending heard again

## Meaning

In Greek choral structure, a responding section corresponding metrically to a strophe; in another rhetorical usage, repetition at the ends of successive units, a figure also commonly called epistrophe.

## Word history

From Greek antistrophē, a counterturn or answering turn, related to anti- and strephein, to turn, with choral and rhetorical developments.

## The responding choral section

In Greek choral form, an antistrophe corresponds metrically to a preceding strophe. The pair organizes patterned response within an ode. This use concerns the architecture of song and its metrical relations; it does not require the sections to repeat identical final words or to express directly opposite propositions.

## The rhetorical terminal return

Some rhetorical traditions use antistrophe for repeated endings, also commonly called epistrophe. “We argued for home, we endured for home, we returned for home” lets a stable ending gather different actions. The recurrence can produce convergence, insistence, or closure, depending on the changing material that leads toward it.

## Historical kinship is not identity

Both uses involve return or response, but their formal criteria differ. A metrically answering choral section and a repeated phrase at clause endings are not the same structure. A good analysis names which tradition and feature are at issue instead of letting the word's broad metaphor replace precise description.

## Listen where the units resolve

Mark the ends of successive clauses and compare their wording. Separately, when studying a choral ode, examine the correspondence of paired sections. Keeping those procedures apart allows the shared idea of a counterturn to remain suggestive without turning two distinct technical usages into an indistinct theory of repetition.

## Further into the word

Beginnings and endings organize attention differently. A repeated opening establishes a platform from which successive statements depart; a repeated ending makes different statements arrive at the same destination. The latter can feel like a verdict accumulating support, or like a refrain that absorbs every attempt to escape it.

The choral usage asks another question: how can one section answer another through corresponding form? Metrical relation can establish an architectural partnership without semantic repetition. The response may develop, complicate, or redirect what came before, so “answering” should not be reduced to simple agreement.

For a useful experiment, rewrite three parallel clauses first with the same beginning and then with the same ending. Note whether the emphasis feels launched or gathered.
