# 045 · différance

Meaning differs, and its arrival is deferred.

## Definition

Derrida's deliberately altered spelling naming the intertwined differentiation and deferral through which signs signify, challenging the idea that meaning originates in a fully present, self sufficient foundation or final signified.

## Etymology

Derrida modifies French différence using différer, which can mean both to differ and to defer; the changed vowel is not pronounced differently.

## Meaning In Motion

A word becomes intelligible through differences from other words, while explaining it sends us onward to further signs rather than delivering meaning outside signification. Derrida's spelling joins these spatial and temporal movements without naming a simple object or ultimate cause behind them.

## The Crucial Distinction

The substituted letter is visible in writing but inaudible in standard French pronunciation, unsettling a familiar preference for speech. Différance does not mean that every interpretation is equally defensible. It questions pure presence and final closure while leaving particular readings answerable to textual differences, contexts, and traces.

## Trace Without A Lost Origin

To call a mark a trace is not simply to say that it records an earlier, fully present object. It bears relations to what is absent from its immediate appearance. The past and anticipated future of a word participate in what seems to be its present meaning.

## Reading An Opposition

Consider calling one version of a story the original and another a copy. The original becomes identifiable through the possibility of repetition, quotation, and alteration. Reading this dependency closely complicates its apparent independence without claiming that the two versions have identical histories or authority.

## Original literary sentence

Each footprint explained the traveler by another absence, while the road, postponing its promised arrival, borrowed its direction from crossroads that were already elsewhere and not yet finished departing.

## Extended reading

Try defining a simple word without using another sign. A gesture, an example, and an image may help, but each still operates through relations and context. This does not mean communication never succeeds. It means the imagined escape into perfectly self present meaning cannot simply be assumed as the hidden guarantee of every successful exchange.

The temporal dimension matters as much as the relational one. Understanding unfolds through what has already been marked and what further context may disclose. A sentence's early words change significance as its later words arrive. This everyday observation is only an entry point, not an exhaustive account of Derrida's intervention.

A disciplined exercise follows one opposition in a passage: inside and outside, original and copy, or speech and writing. Ask whether the allegedly secondary term is required to articulate the primary one. The answer must arise from the passage's workings, not from mechanically reversing the hierarchy. The sculptural plates model dependence and delay; they do not depict a mysterious substance called différance sitting behind language. The word names a challenge to that very expectation of a final hidden object.
